BACKSTAGE 2.0 — IMAGE / DATA NOTES
Research snapshot: 27 Sep 2026

ARTIST IMAGES
Most artist images use current public Spotify artist profile images (spotifycdn / i.scdn.co).
Exceptions use public promo imagery where a stable current Spotify URL was not surfaced.
The app always renders initials behind the remote image, so a failed image never creates a blank placeholder.

EVENT ARTWORK
Real public event artwork is referenced remotely from official venue, promoter, Ticketportal/Ticketmaster or artist/label sources where found.
Events without a verified poster intentionally use the artist profile image and are labelled ARTIST PHOTO.

IMPORTANT
Remote imagery requires an internet connection and may be changed or removed by the source site. BACKSTAGE 2.0 does not cache third-party artwork in the service worker.
